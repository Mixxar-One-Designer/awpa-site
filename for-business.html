Marketplace

How an escrow-backed sale actually moves

This is the sequence every order follows, from listing to payout, so both sides know exactly where their money and their goods stand at each step.

01

List the product

A business posts a product with a price and description for buyers to find and message about.

02

Buyer pays into escrow

The buyer pays securely inside awpa. Funds don't go to the seller yet, they're held by awpa.

03

Seller delivers

The business ships or hands over the product, knowing payment is already secured.

04

Buyer confirms

Once the buyer has the product and is satisfied, they confirm delivery inside the chat.

05

Funds released

Only after confirmation does awpa release the held payment to the seller.

If something goes wrong, either the buyer or the seller can open a dispute before funds are released. awpa reviews the dispute and decides how the held payment is handled.
